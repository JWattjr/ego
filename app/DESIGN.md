World: a clear city-walk companion. Chalk white surfaces, deep ink text, cobalt money, quiet sage safety cues. Wallet is the first-screen working assumption. Grounded candidate 6 is a public-walk pocket guide; the skill's direction seed was consulted, while the explicit product palette and function govern the result.

Signature: coin gently turns into a collectible after a confirmed claim; hold progress is tactile and cancelable, never automatic. Reduced motion removes coin movement. Map circles encode uncertainty; exact marker appears only after the distance check.
